## 3. Comprehensive Service Architecture (12 Industrial Disciplines)

Stencil lists 12 services with sparse, repetitive descriptions. Picasa will feature **12 fully developed commercial disciplines** with exact technical deliverables, output formats, and target industries:

### 1. Hand-Crafted Pen Tool Clipping Path
* **Original Description:** Mathematical Bezier vector curves hand-drawn inside Adobe Photoshop with smooth 0.5px interior offsets to prevent edge fringing and background bleed.
* **Deliverables:** Layered PSD with active Work Path, Transparent PNG, Pure White RGB 255 JPG.
* **Target:** E-Commerce brands, catalog publishers, packshot photographers.

### 2. Multi-Part Clipping Path & Color Swatching
* **Original Description:** Complex multi-layer path dissection isolating individual components (collar, buttons, fabric panels, soles, hardware) for independent recoloring and automated web swatching.
* **Deliverables:** Multi-path PSD with named vector paths per material segment.
* **Target:** Fashion apparel, footwear manufacturers, multi-colorway product lines.

### 3. Ghost Mannequin & Collar Joint Synthesis
* **Original Description:** Seamless anatomical assembly merging multiple garment angles (exterior front + interior neck collar/lining) into a realistic 3D hollow-body fit without visible mannequin rigs.
* **Deliverables:** Symmetrical garment outline, inner shadow depth, color-balanced tag alignment.
* **Target:** Apparel retailers, luxury fashion houses, Lookbook creators.

### 4. Advanced Alpha Channel & Soft Edge Masking
* **Original Description:** Micro-strand pixel extraction designed for complex boundaries where vector paths fail: fine flying hair, animal fur, transparent glass, tulle, and lace.
* **Deliverables:** Non-destructive 16-bit Alpha Channel mask, edge de-contamination layer.
* **Target:** Fashion editorial, beauty campaigns, model portfolios.

### 5. High-End Commercial & Editorial Retouching
* **Original Description:** Non-destructive frequency separation preserving authentic skin pores while eliminating blemishes, stray hairs, fabric creases, dust specs, and optical sensor spots.
* **Deliverables:** High-frequency texture layer + low-frequency tone layer in 16-bit PSD.
* **Target:** Beauty advertising, luxury editorial, commercial lifestyle photography.

### 6. Photorealistic Shadow Synthesis & Mirror Reflection
* **Original Description:** Natural drop shadow, cast perspective shadow, and floating ambient occlusion tailored to light source physics, anchoring products onto surfaces without artificial muddying.
* **Deliverables:** Isolated shadow layer with adjustable opacity on transparent backing.
* **Target:** Footwear, cosmetics, electronics, home decor packshots.

### 7. Spectrophotometric Color Matching & Grading
* **Original Description:** Precise color correction aligning product digital files with physical fabric Pantone swatches, neutralizing studio lighting color cast and ensuring print/screen consistency.
* **Deliverables:** Calibrated sRGB, AdobeRGB (1998), and CMYK profiles.
* **Target:** Textiles, furniture, cosmetics, global brand compliance.

### 8. Luxury Jewelry & Facet Macro Retouching
* **Original Description:** Specialized macro post-production: diamond brilliance enhancement, reflection cleanup on high-polish metals, gemstone saturation balance, and micro-scratch removal.
* **Deliverables:** Pristine high-contrast jewel facets, crisp metallic edges, natural glint.
* **Target:** Fine jewelry brands, watchmakers, luxury auction houses.

### 9. Raster-to-Vector (CAD & Vectorizing Engine)
* **Original Description:** Manual mathematical tracing of low-resolution sketches, logos, technical blueprints, and artwork into infinitely scalable SVG, EPS, and AI vector assets.
* **Deliverables:** Clean Adobe Illustrator AI, EPS, SVG with optimized anchor counts.
* **Target:** Screen printers, packaging designers, corporate identity teams.

### 10. 360-Degree Turntable Spin Sequence Alignment
* **Original Description:** Batch frame-by-frame center-axis stabilization, uniform exposure normalization, and consistent clipping path isolation across 24, 36, or 72 turntable frames.
* **Deliverables:** Sequenced WebP/PNG frames ready for interactive 360-spin web viewers.
* **Target:** Automotive parts, consumer electronics, 3D interactive web stores.

### 11. Architectural & Interior HDR Exposure Fusion
* **Original Description:** Manual multi-bracket window pull, perspective vertical alignment, color cast neutralization, and ambient interior warmth balancing for luxury real estate listings.
* **Deliverables:** Balanced interior/exterior view, corrected vertical keystoning.
* **Target:** Real estate developers, architectural studios, hospitality groups.

### 12. Commercial Motion & Video Post-Production
* **Original Description:** Professional e-commerce short-form video editing, background wire removal, color grading (DaVinci Resolve), audio sweetening, and vertical aspect re-framing (Reels/TikTok).
* **Deliverables:** 4K / 1080p60 ProRes 422, H.264, WebM masters.
* **Target:** Fashion reels, Amazon video ads, social commerce campaigns.
